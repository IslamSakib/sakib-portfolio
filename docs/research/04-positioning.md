What buyers are shown everywhere:
- process steps
- an agency-only or owner-only path
- prices or "from" figures
- named reviews

What's missing in the white-label results: **a named senior developer with public case studies, measurable speed results, and stated overlap hours.** Teams hide the developer; solo sites rarely target agencies.

## Positioning: direct clients

| | |
|---|---|
| Target buyer | Founder, marketing lead or operations owner at a US/UK/AU company whose WordPress site brings in revenue, with no senior WordPress developer of their own |
| Main problem | The site is slow, keeps breaking, needs a relaunch, or nobody looks after it since the last developer left |
| Offer | Find the cause and fix it, then keep it healthy on a monthly plan. Custom builds and relaunches when needed |
| Outcome | A site that loads fast, stays up and can be changed without fear; numbers you can check yourself |
| Reasons to trust | TubeOnAI (downtime ended, 5 s → 1.5 s), Tabby (built and still maintained; founder recommendation), before/after with Google's own tool, written plan before work |
| Differentiation | One senior developer, not a ticket queue; fixes at the cause; overlaps with US and UK working hours; replies to urgent problems within 24 hours |
| Primary CTA | "Get a free site check" (existing audit flow) → "Start a project" |
| Line | "Your WordPress site earns you money. I keep it fast and working." |

## Positioning: agency partners

| | |
|---|---|
| Target agency | 2–25 person design or marketing agency in the US, UK, Canada or Australia that sells WordPress sites without a senior developer |
| Problem | Signed work and nobody reliable to build it; past freelancers missed dates, went quiet, or shipped slow sites that broke tracking |
| Partnership model | White-label: per-site builds from Figma, hourly overflow, or a monthly block of hours. NDA, no contact with your client, your tools |
| Capabilities | Figma to WordPress (Gutenberg/ACF or Elementor), custom plugins, WooCommerce and payments, speed and Core Web Vitals, fixes and care for your clients' sites |
| Communication | Works in your Slack/Asana/Trello; overlap with US/UK hours; reply within 24 hours; staging link for review; handoff notes on every job |
| Reasons to trust | Musemind (design agency) recommendation for 3 months of subcontracted Elementor work; Tabby and TubeOnAI built from Figma; speed results |
| Primary CTA | "Start with a small paid trial project" → agency inquiry form |
| Line | "Your design, built in WordPress under your name, on your date." |

Claims allowed (approved commitments only): maintenance plans, 24 h reply on urgent fixes, overlap with US/UK hours, published starting prices. No 24/7, no guarantees of rankings, no "results in X days".
