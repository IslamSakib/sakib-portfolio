# Strategy: sakib.mywrkbase.com (16 Oct 2026)

Data: `seo/01-research/keywords-raw.csv` (95 keywords; Semrush US, 14–16 Oct 2026). Scoring follows Ahrefs' framework: business potential **BP** 0–3, ranking potential **RP** 0–3, traffic **T** 0–3, with `priority = 2×BP + RP + T` (full table in `priorities.csv`).
- **RP** is set for the site's current authority (AS 2): KD < 15 → 3, 15–29 → 2, 30–44 → 1, 45+ or a marketplace SERP → 0.

## Positioning (unchanged, approved 14 Oct)
A senior solo WordPress developer who keeps revenue-earning sites fast and working for direct clients, and builds and cares for sites under agencies' names.

## Topics to own
1. **White-label WordPress for agencies:** development, support, maintenance.
2. **WordPress maintenance and care plans:** including support, emergency fixes and repair.
3. **WordPress speed optimisation service.**
4. **Custom WordPress / WooCommerce development:** builds, relaunches, plugins.

## Findings that change the plan
1. **"Care plans" is a missing vocabulary cluster.** "wordpress care plans" gets 1,000/mo at KD 12, "care plan" 720 at KD 12, "maintenance company" 590 at KD 7. The maintenance page targets "maintenance services" (KD 34) and never says "care plan". wpbuffs ranks #1–3 with plan and package wording.
2. **White-label support is nearly free.** "white label wordpress support" gets 480/mo at **KD 1**, and "white label web development" 1,000 at KD 9. Both are owned by one offshore shop (e2msolutions #1–#5). The agency page should use both phrases.
3. **The speed page targets the harder phrase.** Its focus is "WordPress speed optimization" (KD 34, mixed intent). The service variants are KD 7–18 and are exactly what wpjohnny's single page wins (27 terms in the top 5). Switch the focus to "WordPress speed optimization service".
4. **Fix and repair intent has no service page.** "malware removal service" (320, KD 11), "website repair" (210, KD 3) and "emergency support" (170, KD 10) are commercial and urgent, about 700/mo together. Today they reach only blog posts or the general maintenance page.
5. **Cannibalization (2 pairs):**
   - `/work/tabby/` and `/blog/figma-to-wordpress-handoff/` both target "Figma to WordPress".
   - `/blog/what-wordpress-maintenance-includes/` targets "WordPress maintenance", which overlaps the maintenance service page.
6. **Authority is the ceiling.**
   - The site: AS 2, 93 referring domains (root mywrkbase.com).
   - Competitors: AS 28–42, with 2.5K–3.8K referring domains.
   - KD 30+ targets ("maintenance services", "maintenance plans", "support services") stay bets until Phase 9 adds links.
