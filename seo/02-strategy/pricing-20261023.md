# Pricing check, 23 Oct 2026

Why: the owner said the site prices looked unreal, and that web application development was missing as a service.

Market evidence (agency and vendor blogs, not independent surveys; treat as ranges):
- US freelance full-stack developers: about $70 to $135 an hour mid-level (secondtalent.com/cost-to-hire/united-states, index.dev/blog/freelance-developer-rates, jobbers.io rate database).
- Custom small-business website: about $3,000 to $10,000 (wapiti.digital, ueni.com, webstacks.com); custom brochure design $2,000 to $10,000; ecommerce $5,000 to $50,000.
- Web app MVP: about $10,000 to $50,000 at agencies (gurutechnolabs.com, empat.tech, sam-solutions.com); a coded single-platform MVP often starts near $30,000.
- WordPress: custom theme $3,000 to $10,000, custom plugin $2,000 to $15,000 (folio3, monocubed, webpopdesign).
- Care plans: small-business plans $100 to $300 a month, full range $50 to $500+ (codeable via bsscommerce.com, websults.com).
- Bangladesh freelancers: about $16 to $62 an hour, median about $31 (projectcostestimator.com, South Asia band).

Decision (src/prices.js): solo developer in Dhaka serving US/UK/AU buyers prices at the low end of the Western range and well above local rates, about $60 an hour equivalent.
- Custom website from $3,500 (typical $3,500 to $9,000); online store with dashboard from $8,000 (typical $8,000 to $20,000)
- Web application first version from $9,000 (typical $9,000 to $30,000)
- WordPress theme build from $3,000, WooCommerce from $4,500, custom plugin from $1,200
- Website care $149 and $299 a month (unchanged, inside the $100 to $300 band); repair from $150, speed from $450, malware from $199
- Extra developer time $60 an hour; white-label $45 an hour or from $1,500 per site (was $35 and $1,200)
