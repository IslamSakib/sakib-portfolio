# Full-stack pivot: keyword decisions (23 Oct 2026)

Sources: Semrush US database via shared Access-4 (23 Oct 2026), GSC 28 days (gsc-20261023.md). Volumes are monthly US searches.

## Finding that changes the location pages
"Full stack developer + state" phrases are near zero: USA 20, Florida 10, California 20, New York 20, Texas 0, "best full stack developer" 20. Nobody searches them. Real US demand sits in:
- "web development company" + place: USA 590 (KD 10), New York 720, Texas 210, Florida 170 (KD 11), Miami 140, California 110.
- "website developer" + city: Houston 260, Chicago 210, San Diego 210, Denver 170, Tampa 90, Orlando 70.
- Head terms: website development company 9,900 (KD 19), custom web development 3,600 (KD 20), custom website development 3,600 (KD 50), hire full stack developer 880 (KD 22).

Decision: the location pages keep Sakib's requested phrase in the title and H1 ("Full Stack Developer in Florida"), and also target the volume phrase in the body and H2s ("web development company Florida", "custom website development Florida"). USA hub leads with "hire full stack developer" and "web development company USA". Keep the set small (hub + 10) because the phrases are low volume; add more only after Search Console shows impressions.
