1. **/blog/wordpress-maintenance-cost/**: price ranges with sources; suits business and small-business blogs.
2. **/blog/best-wordpress-maintenance-services/**: five providers compared with their own published prices; suits roundups.
3. **/blog/wordpress-white-screen-of-death/** and **/blog/wordpress-plugin-conflict/**: how-to fixes; suit WordPress resource pages.
4. **/blog/figma-to-wordpress-handoff/**: suits design-community roundups.
5. **Proposed new asset (not built):** an original study, for example "How fast are 100 WordPress agency sites on mobile?" using local Lighthouse. Original data is the strongest link magnet. Needs the owner's yes.

Rule: no paid links, no link swaps at scale, no PBNs.
