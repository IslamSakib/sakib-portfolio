## Takeaways
1. **One page per cluster wins.** wpjohnny ranks for 27 speed terms with one page, and e2m ranks for most white-label terms with one or two. That supports the current page map rather than many thin pages.
2. **The maintenance page misses the "plans/packages/care plan" vocabulary.** It's a separate wording cluster from "maintenance services" (wpbuffs ranks #1–3 for it). KD for "care plan(s)" is only 12.
3. **White-label support and maintenance** (480 and 260, KD 1–9) are almost free wins. Both are owned by one offshore shop, and the site already has a white-label maintenance post.
4. **Authority gap.** Competitors hold 2.5K–3.8K referring domains against the site's 93 (root). Phase 9 matters for the KD 30+ terms.
