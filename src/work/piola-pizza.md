## The brief

Piola Pizza is a Neapolitan pizzeria in Esbjerg, Denmark. It needed a site where customers could order online quickly from a phone, and where the menu and deals could change without a developer.

## What I built

- A WooCommerce ordering system with a dynamic menu and combo deals
- A mobile-first checkout kept as short as possible
- Customer accounts for repeat orders and order tracking
- Promotional banners for seasonal and daily deals
- Local SEO so the site is found by people nearby
