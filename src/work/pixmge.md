## The brief

Pixmge is an image editing, animation and digital marketing agency. It needed a site that explains its services clearly, shows its work, and turns visitors into enquiries without friction.

## What I built

- Service sections with clear pricing
- A short quote request and client onboarding flow
- A portfolio built to show visual work well
- A fast, secure build the agency can grow on

## The result

- Delivered on schedule
- The quote request system was live and receiving enquiries from day one

## What is on the site today

Checked on the live site on 11 October 2026.

- A page for each service, from background removal and clipping paths to jewelry editing and vector conversion
- Pricing, a user guide, an FAQ and a blog
- English and German versions
- WordPress with Elementor and Rank Math
