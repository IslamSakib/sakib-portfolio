## The brief

Pixmge is an image editing, animation and digital marketing agency. It needed a site that explains its services clearly, shows its work, and turns visitors into enquiries without friction.

## What I built

- Service sections with clear pricing
- A short quote request and client onboarding flow
- A portfolio built to show visual work well
- A fast, secure build the agency can grow on

## The result

- Delivered on schedule
- The quote request system was live and receiving enquiries from day one

## What the agency website had to do

An agency sells services people cannot see until they ask. So each service has its own page that explains it, pricing is shown openly, and the path from reading to asking for a quote is short.

## Inside the site, 11 October 2026

Read from the live site, page by page.

**Size.** 35 pages in the sitemap.

**What the pages do.**

- A page for each of the 13 services: background removal, clipping path, image masking, drop shadow, reflection shadow, invisible mannequin, color adjustment, photo retouching, product photo editing, model retouching, motion design, jewelry editing and vector conversion
- Each service page follows one pattern: the service, the work in action, then a quote request
- A pricing page with two plans: pay-as-you-go from $1.00 an image, and a quoted enterprise plan
- An offer of the first 5 images free
- A shop, cart, checkout and account area for ordering
- English and German versions

**How it is built.**

- WordPress with Elementor on the GeneratePress theme
- Rank Math for search
- Served through Cloudflare with Brotli compression

**What search engines see.**

- Structured data for the organisation, the website and each page
- Every one of the 96 images on the home page has alt text, and 84 of them load only when scrolled to
