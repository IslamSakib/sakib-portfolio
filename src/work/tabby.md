## The brief

Tabby is an AI accountant for small businesses and the self-employed. The team had finished designs in Figma and a tight deadline. They needed a marketing site that could launch in weeks and carry far more than pages: a tax calculator, a help center, payments and analytics.

## What I built

- The full site from the Figma designs, responsive on every screen size
- A custom tax calculator with dynamic logic
- Custom plugins for the AI demos and dynamic content
- WooCommerce with subscription payments
- A help center, blog and product updates on one content structure
- CRM, Mixpanel and Crisp Chat integrations
- Security audits and vulnerability patching

## The 2.0 launch

When Tabby released version 2.0, I built the entire new website. The condition was simple: the new site had to keep the search visibility the old one had earned.

## The result

- Launched on schedule
- Core Web Vitals above 90
- I still maintain and update the site today

## What is on the site today

Checked on the live site on 11 October 2026.

- Pages for each kind of customer: consultants and coaches, contractors, licensed professionals and real estate professionals
- Four free calculators: 1099 tax, billable hour, tax bracket and tax refund
- Comparison pages against QuickBooks and Wave, a help center, a blog and a partner program
- WordPress with Elementor, Rank Math for search and one caching layer
