Landing page vs website comes down to one question: how many things do you want the visitor to do? A landing page asks for one. A website lets people explore.

## What each one is for

| | Landing page | Website |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | One action, such as an enquiry | Many: learn, compare, contact |
| Pages | One | Many |
| Traffic from | An ad, an email, a launch | Search, referrals, direct visits |
| Navigation | None or minimal | Full menu |
| Best for | A campaign or a single offer | The whole business |

## When a landing page is enough

- You are running paid ads for one offer.
- You are launching a product or an event.
- You want to test an offer before building a full site.

## When you need a website

- People search for your business by name.
- You have several services, a team and a story to tell.
- You want to rank for more than one keyword.

## How to decide

Start from the traffic. Visitors from a specific ad should land on a page that matches that ad. Everyone else needs a site they can look around. In both cases the page must load fast on a phone and make the next step obvious.
