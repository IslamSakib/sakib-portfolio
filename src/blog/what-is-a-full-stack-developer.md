What is a full stack developer? Someone who builds both sides of a website or web app. The **frontend** is what visitors see and click: pages, forms, the shop. The **backend** is what runs behind it: the database, user accounts, payments, emails and the admin screens your team uses. "Full stack" means one person can do both, so a project does not stall between a designer, a frontend developer and a backend developer.

## What does a full stack developer actually build?

Take Mayabee, an online shop I built for a handbag and jewellery brand in Dhaka. One developer made all of this:

- The storefront: shop, filters, product pages, cart and checkout, in English and Bangla
- The PHP and MySQL backend that stores products, orders and customers
- The admin dashboard: orders, phone orders, delivery and cash on delivery, stock, reports and four staff roles

If those had been three people, every change to checkout would have needed a meeting. With one developer, a change to the order form and the dashboard screen that shows it ships together.

## When is a full stack developer the right hire?

Hire one when:

- Your project is a business website, a store, a booking system or a first version of a product
- You want one person to ask when something is wrong
- The design, the code and the admin side need to agree with each other

Hire a team instead when the product is large, needs several specialists working in parallel, or has a long roadmap with many releases at once.

## What should a full stack developer show you?

Ask for live projects you can click through, not screenshots. Then check how fast they load. Google's published limits for a good page are a Largest Contentful Paint of 2.5 seconds or less, Interaction to Next Paint of 200 milliseconds or less and a Cumulative Layout Shift of 0.1 or less (source: [Google web.dev, Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals)). You can test any site yourself on [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/).

## What I would check before hiring anyone

1. Can they show a project like yours, live?
2. Do they build the admin side as well as the website?
3. Will you own the code, the design files and the logins?
4. How quickly do they reply, and in which hours?
