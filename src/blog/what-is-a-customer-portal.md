A customer portal is a secure part of your website where each customer signs in to see their own information: orders, documents, requests, invoices or progress. It replaces the emails that ask for a status or a file.

## Signs you need one

- Staff answer the same status question many times a week.
- Files travel by email and get lost.
- Customers share one login, or have none.
- You cannot see which customers are waiting on you.

## What a portal includes

1. **Individual sign-in** with password reset.
2. **Roles.** Customer, staff and admin see different things.
3. **A status view** for orders, requests or projects.
4. **Documents and messages** attached to the right customer.
5. **A staff view** with who is waiting and what is done.

## What it costs

A first version starts at $999 and usually lands between $999 and $4,000 in US dollars, with the price in writing before work starts. Open Alliance Learning, built on separate dashboards for learners, teachers and assessors, is an example of roles done properly.

Start with one customer group and one view. Add the rest once people use it. See [customer portal development](/web-application-development/customer-portal-development/) to send a brief.
